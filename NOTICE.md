# Third-party material

## OpenStreetMap data
`inputs/utp_graph.graphml`, `experiments/E_network/inputs_um/utp_graph.graphml` and the copies inside `archives/` are road
networks derived from OpenStreetMap. © OpenStreetMap contributors. Available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL) 1.0,
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright. The second-network snapshot date and query are recorded in
`experiments/E_network/inputs_um/network_manifest.json`.

## ESOGU-ML5EV dataset
`experiments/F_road_load/calibration/ESOGU-ML5EV-v1.0.0.zip` (and its copy inside the Experiment F archive) is the unmodified
ESOGU-ML5EV dataset v1.0.0, Eskişehir Osmangazi University, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14912892, described in
Polat et al. (2025), Data in Brief, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12340509/. It is redistributed under the Apache
License 2.0; the dataset's own LICENSE file is retained inside the archive (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).
It was used only to inform the road-load sensitivity range of Experiment F (`experiments/F_road_load/calibration/METHOD.md`).
