## Sources and scope

- Polat et al. (2025), *A dataset for state of charge and range estimation of an L5 type electric vehicle that is used for Urban Logistic*, Data in Brief, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12340509/ . Data DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14912892 . Original Apache-2.0 archive included unchanged (published MD5 25fee94466835e59043edeb708e44ed2).
- Musoshi manufacturer lists 700 kg curb mass for Pop-Up Mini, https://www.musoshi.com/pop-up-mini-technical-details . That analysis assigns different total masses; we use the data-originating paper's explicit 300 kg additional cargo, manufacturer curb mass, and an explicitly assumed 75 kg driver. Repeat coefficient inversion for 50 and 100 kg driver mass. Exact tested mass not measured in the archive.

The archive contains 35 trip files/14,658 rows. The differently named 25UMW2 and 35UCW1 files are exact duplicates; both excluded from fitting. 25LCW2 timestamps are minute-resolution/duplicate rather than usable 1-s timestamps; excluded. Other speeds and summer trips are outside the diagnostic design. Summer lacks slope. Nothing is deleted or modified in raw data. `file_audit.csv` records each file and valid one-second intervals; its energy totals are partial when timing is incomplete. Do not treat them as total-trip values for irregular files.

## Measurement and replay definition

Analyse the eight 15 km/h winter trips, both direction and cargo conditions, W1 training and W2 held-out repeat files. Use consecutive 1-s intervals with finite speed and electrical channels; both endpoint speeds 10–22 km/h, both recorded slopes exactly zero (the paper's slope unit is inconsistently described; zero avoids degree/percent conversion), absolute speed change at most 0.25 m/s per second. Recorded slope zero is a proxy, not a guarantee of physically level terrain. The design selects slow, near-steady segments compatible with the simulator's 22 km/h speed cap. 254 training and 179 held-out intervals; not independent observations.

Gross measured DC-link energy is trapezoidal integration of max(0, voltage*current) over included seconds, divided by 3600. Negative current is not ignored in the audit (net and gross reported separately); gross draw excludes recovered energy to match the no-regeneration model boundary as closely as possible. DC-link is not the whole battery terminal boundary, so residuals may include electrical boundary differences, grade uncertainty and motor/drivetrain losses. No attempt to infer physically separate Crr, eta, drag and auxiliary power from these data.

Replay the flat-road longitudinal model on observed midpoint speed v, interval acceleration a, measured time (1 s) and assumed mass m. Ehat = [max(0, m*g*Crr + 0.615*v^2 + 1.2*m*a)*v/0.90 + 50]/3600 Wh per interval. Fixed coefficients mirror frozen UGV model assumptions, not measured properties of Musoshi: g=9.81; drag force 0.615*v²; rotational mass factor 1.2; efficiency 0.9; auxiliary 50 W. This is an approximate trace replay, not execution of the simulator's internal stop-to-stop motion policy. Solve the per-trip aggregate energy equation for effective Crr; average four W1 inversions equally for external diagnostic center. Floor/ceil the range of W1 estimates to multiples of .001 for sensitivity anchors; keep original .012 base. W2 values do not set anchors. No independent GMTLSV measurement or CI. Inspect `trip_replay.csv` and `eligible_intervals.csv` for reproducibility.

## Diagnostic result

Training effective Crr 0.008122–0.019452, equal-trip center 0.012873. Four held-out estimates 0.014892–0.020713; two lie slightly above upper sensitivity anchor .020. Absolute percentage error averaged over four held-out trips: 22.59% with original .012 versus 19.65% with external training center. This is weak external support, not sufficient for calibrated target-vehicle prediction or population inference. Do not claim a directional payload effect from the inverted coefficients. The target study retains original battery, mass, speed, motor and auxiliary model and varies only road resistance in both prediction and physical execution. The sensitivity anchors are not asserted to contain all plausible values or to be a statistical interval. The F campaign is explicitly labelled measurement-anchored sensitivity, not independent validation of the GMTLSV.
