# Exploratory manipulation check (before the protocols were finalised)

The check ran on seeds 901001 and 902001 (excluded from all confirmatory work), with Hungarian, 12 UGVs / 100 requests, 100% SOC, αE 0 and 1, and the probe on. Its purpose was to verify that payload overrides reach the cost and change the energy–duration relation, before committing to Experiment A. These values informed the predictions in `protocols/PROTOCOLS.md` and are not confirmatory evidence.

| Empty mass, passenger mass | ρ | Within-request r (αE = 1 runs) | Decision-level saving of αE (αE = 1 runs) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 825 kg, 80 kg | 7.4% | 0.9971 | 0.39% of run energy |
| 586.5 kg, 80 kg | 9.5% | 0.9946 | 0.57% |
| 455.8 kg, 80 kg | 11.2% | 0.9931 | 0.76% |
| 455.8 kg, 160 kg (non-physical) | 22.4% | 0.9664 | 2.25% |

- The planned energy of the chosen insertions summed to the run's battery withdrawal within 10⁻⁸ %.
- Realized per-seed changes with only two seeds ranged from −5.4% to +0.6%, too noisy to read.
- The 60-run kit self-test (seeds 905001–907001) later showed the decision-level saving's per-run SD is about 0.43 pp, not the 0.06 pp these two seeds suggested.
- The sample-size statement in the protocol was corrected before any confirmatory run. No hypothesis, margin, level or seed range was changed.
